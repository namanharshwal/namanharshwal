### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Robotics Engineer** and **ROS2 Full-Stack Developer** passionate about architecting intelligent, reliable, and real-time autonomous systems. Currently engineering software for serving humanoid mobile robots at **Robot Stem Point, New Delhi**.

- 🔭 **Current Focus**: Autonomous Navigation (Nav2), 3D SLAM (RTAB-Map, OctoMap), and Edge AI on NVIDIA Jetson Orin.
- 🎓 **Education**: B.Tech in Robotics & Automation, **NIMS University** (2022 – 2026).
- 💼 **Industry Role**: Robotics Software Engineer Intern @ **Robot Stem Point** — Building next-gen serving humanoid mobile robots.
- 🔬 **Research & Interests**: Humanoid Robotics, Imitation Learning, Multi-Robot Collaborative Swarms, and Edge AI Computer Vision.
- 💬 **Ask me about**: ROS2 (Humble/Jazzy), MoveIt2, micro-ROS on ESP32/STM32, RealSense & Orbecc depth sensing, and SolidWorks CAD design.
